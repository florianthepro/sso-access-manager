Empfohlen, interaktiv mit curl:
sh -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/florianthepro/sso-access-manager/main/install.sh)"

Alternativ mit wget:
sh -c "$(wget -qO- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/florianthepro/sso-access-manager/main/install.sh)"

Anderer Zielordner:
SSOAC_DIR="$HOME/ssoac" sh -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/florianthepro/sso-access-manager/main/install.sh)"
